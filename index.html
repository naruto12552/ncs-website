About the School

A School Built on
Purpose & Excellence

Narkatiaganj Central School (NCS) stands as one of Narkatiaganj's most trusted educational institutions. Situated at Hardiya Chowk, we have been nurturing young minds with a complete English-medium environment backed by experienced and dedicated faculty.

Founded in 2010 under the leadership of Mr. Afroz Akhtar, NCS has grown into one of West Champaran's most respected English-medium schools. Our Digital Smart Classrooms bring learning to life, while free tutorial programmes ensure no child is left behind — regardless of economic background.

We take special pride in our Girl Child Initiative — providing guidance, mentorship and a safe learning environment that empowers every young woman. From Art & Craft Exhibitions to Science Fairs, our students regularly dazzle dignitaries and guests at school events.
